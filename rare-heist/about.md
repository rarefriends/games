# Rare Heist

Your Rare Friend is the thief, drawn in its original on-chain pixels.

Steal the trophy and reach the exit in a turn-based cutaway vault. Security moves only when you do. Learn in five short lessons, then tackle fourteen jobs.

## How to play

Arrow keys move and climb. Space waits, E uses a vent, Q fires an EMP and L switches lights. Tap a cell to walk there. INSPECT shows hazards without spending a turn. UNDO turns a run into practice. WATCH SOLUTION plays a stored clean route.

Progress and goals follow your Friend through the game server.

## Daily Heist

One vault per UTC day. Paid entry (150 RF for three ranked attempts) is coming soon. Until then the archive offers free practice and solutions for the last 7 closed UTC days.

## Shop and Last Heist

Hatchwork and Signal Paper themes cost 250 RF each, Golden Trail costs 500 RF, and The Black Archive adds three heists for 2,500 RF. Payments use the platform confirmation dialog.

Last Heist is a free shared challenge: clear the current vault, hold it, then add one wall and prove a clean way out. No RF stakes.
